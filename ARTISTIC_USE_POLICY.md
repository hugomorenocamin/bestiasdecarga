# Artistic Use Policy

This document clarifies the intended use of this project beyond the legal text of its license.

## Intent

This project is an open artistic system designed for reproduction, study, and reinterpretation within artistic and academic contexts.

It is not intended to function as a commercial product, kit, or manufacturable device.

## Permitted Uses

- Non-commercial reproduction of the system

- Educational and research use

- Artistic modification and derivative works

- Exhibition of derived works in cultural, artistic, and academic contexts

## Not Permitted Without Explicit Permission

- Commercial manufacturing or industrial reproduction of the device

- Sale of assembled units

- Sale of kits or partial kits

- Sale of design files (STL, PCB, schematics, firmware)

- Use of the design as a commercial product or embedded component in a product

## On Derivative Works

Artistic reinterpretations are encouraged.

However, derivative works must:

- Be conceptually and materially distinct from the original system as a device

- Maintain attribution to the original author

- Be released under the same license terms when distributed

## Contact for Artistic Use

If you intend to use any part of this project in an artistic context that involves public exhibition, commissioning, or institutional support, please contact the author in advance to clarify terms and attribution:

Email: hugomoreno311@gmail.com

## Closing Statement

This project understands the body as a site of measurement, labour, and extraction.

Its openness is intended as a critical gesture, not as a pathway to commodification.
