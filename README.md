# Bestias de carga
## Overview

This project is an artistic system that sonifies respiratory and cardiac signals in real time.
It operates as a critical reflection on labour, self-exploitation, and the physiological dimension of contemporary neoliberal production regimes.

The body is treated here not as metaphor, but as an operational site of measurement, extraction, and feedback.

The system translates physiological signals into sound, generating a direct physical representation of internal bodily rhythms under stress, attention, and exertion.

## System Structure

### The installation is composed of four interconnected physical modules:

- Part 01 – emitter

- Part 02 – receiver

- Part 03 – respiration belt

- Part 04 – solenoid-driven mallet

### Each module contains:

- Mechanical design and structural drawings

- 3D printable components (.stl)

- Electronics schematics and PCB design

- Firmware (Arduino-based control system)

## Artistic Intent

This work operates at the intersection of bio-sensing and sound art.

It questions:

- the quantification of the body under self-optimization logics

- the feedback loops between attention, labour, and exhaustion

The system is not a medical device, nor a fitness product. It is an artistic instrument.
